Last updated更新日期

Scope适用范围

This policy covers this support website and the apps identified below. Where an app has a separate privacy notice, consult that notice for its data practices.本政策说明本支持网站和下方列出的 App 如何处理数据。设有专属隐私政策的 App，请参阅对应政策。

When you visit this site访问网站时

The site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This site stores your selected language in your browser so that your preference carries over to other pages. You can clear it in your browser’s site data settings.本网站使用 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访客的 IP 地址。网站会将你选择的语言保存在浏览器中，以便在其他页面沿用。你可以在浏览器的网站数据设置中清除此偏好。

When you email support通过邮件反馈时

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address, your message and any attachments through the email provider. These details are used to investigate your request and reply. Please remove personal details unrelated to the issue from screenshots or logs.发送邮件后，开发者会通过邮件服务商收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，用于了解问题并回复。发送前，请移除截图或日志中与问题无关的个人信息。

Retention and deletion保留与删除

Support correspondence is kept for the time needed to handle your request and follow-up. To ask about or request deletion of your correspondence, contact support from the address you used and identify the conversation.为处理反馈和后续沟通，开发者会在所需期间内保留往来邮件。如需查询或请求删除邮件，请使用原邮箱联系开发者，并指明对应的邮件。
