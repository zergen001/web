Privacy policy 隐私政策

This policy applies to Zergen’s local macOS utilities. You can use them without creating a Zergen account, signing in or providing an email address. 本政策适用于 Zergen 的本地 macOS 工具。你可以直接使用这些工具，无需注册 Zergen 账户、登录或提供邮箱地址。

Files and settings 文件与设置

When you use a tool to work with a file, it processes the file on your Mac. Zergen does not receive the file or its contents. The apps do not use online file processing or sync your data through iCloud. 当你用工具处理文件时，处理过程在你的 Mac 上完成。文件及其内容不会发送给 Zergen。这些工具不使用在线文件处理服务，也不通过 iCloud 同步你的数据。

App preferences are stored on your Mac. You can delete files you save or export in the Finder. To locate or remove a particular app’s stored settings, email us with the app name. App 的偏好设置保存在你的 Mac 上。你可以在「访达」中删除自己保存或导出的文件。如果想查找或清除某款工具的设置，可以把 App 名称发给我们。

macOS permissions macOS 权限

Some features need permission to access a folder or use a system function. You can decline the macOS prompt or review the app’s permissions in System Settings under Privacy & Security. Turning off a permission can stop the feature that depends on it from working. 某些功能需要访问文件夹或使用系统功能。你可以在 macOS 提示时拒绝授权，也可以前往「系统设置」中的「隐私与安全性」查看和更改已授予的权限。关闭权限后，依赖该权限的功能可能无法使用。

Usage and diagnostics 使用统计与诊断

These apps do not include analytics SDKs, track which features you use or automatically send crash reports to Zergen. 这些 App 不集成使用统计 SDK，不跟踪你使用了哪些功能，也不会自动向 Zergen 发送崩溃报告。

When you contact support 当你联系技术支持时

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, such as a screenshot or diagnostic log. We use them to investigate the issue and reply. You can describe the problem without attaching a file. 如果你来信求助，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件正文，以及你主动附上的截图或诊断日志。这些信息用于排查问题和回复邮件。你也可以只用文字描述问题。

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and follow-up. To ask about your information or request deletion of a support conversation, email us from the address you originally used and tell us which conversation you mean. 我们在处理问题和后续沟通所需的期间内保留往来邮件。如需查询相关信息或请求删除某次通信，请用原邮箱来信，并注明对应的邮件。
